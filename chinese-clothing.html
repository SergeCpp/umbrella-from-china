Chinese Clothing — Love at First Sight

Love at First Sight【一见倾心系列】is a Chinese Clothing music slideshow video series created and running by Bei Feng【北风】

The series encompasses several fashion styles in China: Hanfu, Lolita Fashion, JK Uniform, and more. Love at First Sight gained Bilibili Award.
